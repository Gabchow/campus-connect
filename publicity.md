# Publicity

## Publicity channels

- Posters around campus notice boards.
- Social media announcements on the campus account.
- Email announcement to all students.
- Registration link or sign-up form details.
- Reminder post one day before the event.

## Timeline

- Two weeks before: poster design and approval.
- One week before: posters displayed and email sent.
- One day before: final reminder.

## Questions for discussion

- What is the budget for printing posters?
- Assumption: the event will have around 100 students attending.
